Overview

Comprehensive Power BI dashboard providing end-to-end visibility into AI-powered customer support operations, enabling data-driven optimization of resolution times, customer satisfaction, cost efficiency, and operational performance.

Dashboard Architecture

Single-Page Layout with 8 KPI Cards:
- Total Tickets, Avg Resolution Hours, CSAT Score, FCR Rate
- Total Cost EUR, Avg First Response Minutes, Repeat Contact Rate, Escalation Rate

8 Interactive Visualizations

1. Resolution Time Trend by Support Path - Tracks performance across AI-only, AI-to-human, human-only paths
2. FCR Rate by Issue Category - Highlights first-contact resolution success
3. CSAT Distribution by Channel & AI Agent - Channel breakdown with AI agent attribution
4. Resolution Hours by AI Agent & Product - Product-specific performance analysis
5. First Response Time by Product & Issue Category - Matrix visualization for bottlenecks
6. Resolution Hours & CSAT by Issue Category - Correlates resolution time with satisfaction
7. Ticket Volume by AI Agent & Support Path - Workload distribution treemap
8. Ticket Distribution by Customer Tier & Lifecycle - Customer segment analysis

Technical Implementation

Data Model: Star schema with FactSupportTicketsTable, five dimension tables, optimized relationships.

DAX Measures: Power BI best practices - DIVIDE with alternative values, proper CALCULATE filter context, NULL value handling.

Performance Optimizations: Date table marked, auto date/time disabled, incremental refresh, row-level security.

Business Impact

Operational Excellence: Identify bottlenecks, optimize response performance, track escalation patterns.

Financial Efficiency: Analyze AI vs. human cost allocation, quantify repeat contact impact, support ROI calculations.

Quality Assurance: Monitor CSAT scores, track FCR rates, evaluate AI agent consistency.

Conclusion

Mature, analytically rigorous approach to AI-powered customer support analytics, providing insights to optimize operations, control costs, and deliver superior customer experiences.
