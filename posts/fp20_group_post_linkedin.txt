AI Agents Customer Support Analytics Dashboard - Project Submission

Executive Summary

Comprehensive Power BI dashboard monitoring AI agent performance in customer support operations, providing actionable insights into ticket resolution patterns, customer satisfaction, cost efficiency, and operational effectiveness.

Technical Architecture

Data Model:
- Fact Table: FactSupportTicketsTable (23 columns)
- Dimension Tables: DimDate, DimIssue, DimCustomerProfile, DimProduct, DimSupportRoute
- Star schema with single-direction cardinality

Key Performance Indicators (8 metrics):
- Total Tickets, Avg Resolution Hours, CSAT Score, FCR Rate
- Total Cost EUR, Avg First Response Minutes, Repeat Contact Rate, Escalation Rate

Analytical Visualizations (8 charts):

1. Resolution Time Trend by Support Path - Temporal analysis across AI-only, AI-to-human, human-only paths
2. FCR Rate by Issue Category - Identifies highest first-contact resolution success
3. CSAT Distribution by Channel & AI Agent - Channel breakdown with AI agent attribution
4. Resolution Hours by AI Agent & Product - Product-specific performance analysis
5. First Response Time by Product & Issue Category - Matrix visualization for bottlenecks
6. Resolution Hours & CSAT by Issue Category - Correlates resolution time with satisfaction
7. Ticket Volume by AI Agent & Support Path - Workload distribution treemap
8. Ticket Distribution by Customer Tier & Lifecycle - Customer segment analysis

DAX Implementation

Power BI best practices: DIVIDE with alternative values, proper CALCULATE filter context, TRUE() syntax, NULL value handling.

Business Value

- Operational Optimization: Identify bottlenecks in resolution and response times
- Cost Management: Track AI vs. human cost allocation
- Quality Assurance: Monitor CSAT scores and escalation rates
- Strategic Planning: Analyze customer segment patterns
- AI Performance: Evaluate effectiveness across support paths

Technical Considerations

Date table marked as Date Table, integer keys for relationships, auto date/time disabled, row-level security, incremental refresh for last 30 days.

Conclusion

Mature, analytically rigorous foundation for AI-powered customer support operations, enabling data-driven decision-making across operational, financial, and quality dimensions.
